# Railway Electrical Vocational Training

## Overview

This repository documents my vocational training in **Electrical General Services** at **Santragachi Coaching Depot, Kharagpur Division, South Eastern Railway**.

- **Training Period:** 01 June 2026 – 15 June 2026
- **Organization:** South Eastern Railway
- **Location:** Santragachi Coaching Depot, West Bengal
- **Training Area:** Electrical General Services

## Training Activities

### K44 Test Bench for Electrical Component Testing

As part of the vocational training, I worked on the **design of a K44 test bench for electrical component testing**.

The activity involved understanding the requirements of electrical component testing and developing a suitable test-bench arrangement for practical testing applications.

### Manpower Shortage Analysis

Conducted a **manpower shortage analysis at NCG Santragachi Depot** to support the evaluation of operational efficiency.

The activity provided exposure to analyzing operational requirements and understanding how manpower availability can affect depot-level operations.
